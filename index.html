Who We Are

Built on teamwork.
Played with respect.

Big Sky Raptors Airsoft (BSR) is a Billings, Montana airsoft team for players who love tactical, team-driven play — and for anyone curious enough to try it for the first time.

Airsoft is a sport where players use spring, electric, or gas-powered replicas that fire small 6 mm plastic BBs. It rewards communication, movement, and good sportsmanship. Whether you're a seasoned operator or lacing up for your first game, there's a spot on the team for you.

Play Smart

Field Safety Rules

Everyone goes home in one piece. These basics apply to every player, every game.

  1. Eyes & face coveredApproved eye protection — full-face recommended — must be worn at all times in the play area.
  2. Barrel disciplineMuzzle down and safety on outside the play area. Use a barrel cover or bag when off-field.
  3. Call your hitsHonesty is the game. When you're hit, call it, raise your hand, and leave the field.
  4. Mind the limitsFollow the field's velocity and engagement-distance limits. Chrono checks happen before play.
  5. Blind-fire is a noAlways see your target. Never shoot at people who aren't wearing protection.
  6. Respect everyoneBe a good teammate and a good opponent. Zero tolerance for harassment or unsafe behavior.

Age note: Player age minimums and waiver requirements will be posted here — check with us before bringing younger players.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Do I need my own gear?

No. We offer rental M4-style rifles and face masks. Details and pricing coming soon.

What should I wear?

Long sleeves, long pants, sturdy shoes or boots, and layers for Montana weather. Earth tones or camo are great, but not required.

Does airsoft hurt?

You'll feel a small sting when hit, but protective gear and clothing help a lot. Protective eyewear is mandatory, and we recommend full-face coverage.

Is there a minimum age?

Age requirements and waiver details are being finalized. Message us on Facebook and we'll get you an answer.

How do I sign up?

Send us a message on Facebook. We'll get you set up for the next game day.
